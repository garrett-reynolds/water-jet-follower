## Coverage and mounting

Four portrait 16:9 images of 4.5 × 8 ft, overlapping 1 ft at three joins, cover 15 ft: 4 × 4.5 − 3 × 1 = 15 ft [U calculation]. Each diagonal is about 110 inches. Nominal optical throw is about 2 ft at a 0.25 ratio; this is not the required service-bay depth.

Three-unit alternative: three 5.5 × 9.78 ft optical images, overlapping 0.75 ft at two joins, cover 15 ft. Mask the unused height electronically to show 8 ft [U calculation]. Optical diagonals are about 135 inches. Reduced brightness and image density require testing.

Keep 3–3.5 ft as provisional rear-bay design space [U], pending model-specific placement drawings. Rotation moves UST image offset sideways, so check end clearances as well as depth. Mounting compatibility, cooling and service access remain unverified.

## Surface

Test a purpose-made diffuse rear-projection textile such as 122-inch white SoftScreen [V]. Approximately six linear yards allows an 18 ft raw length and finishing margin [U]. The finished visible panel is 15 × 8 ft. Confirm finished dimensions and tensioning with the fabricator. Keep framing out of the projected image and do not add opaque branding directly behind the screen.

## Scope

Excludes main tunnel structure, labour, content, software licences, tracking, sound, spare projector and ongoing support. Assumes an indoor flat screen and controlled lighting. The playback PC allowance includes suitable independent display outputs; adapters are in the cable allowance.
