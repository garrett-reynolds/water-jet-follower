## Coverage

Baseline: two landscape 16:9 images of 8 x 4.5 ft, with 1 ft overlap, give 15 x 4.5 ft combined projection [U calculation]. Each image is approximately 110 inches diagonal. Nominal optical throw is 8 x 0.25 = 2 ft [U calculation]; complete mounting and service bay remains unverified.

A conventional 0.496 short-throw lens needs approximately 3.97 ft optical throw at this width, plus chassis clearance. With less than 4 ft behind the screen, UST remains the candidate approach. Three landscape USTs can reduce nominal throw to about 1.38 ft but give approximately 3.09 ft image height [U calculations].

## Surface and scope

Retain the full-height white wall fabric allowance while projecting onto only a 15 x 4.5 ft band. The image elevation remains a layout choice. Keep the fabric taut and keep intermediate framing out of beam paths. Playback PC needs two independent feeds.

Excludes main tunnel structure, labour, animation, software licences, tracking, audio, spare projector and support. All non-projector amounts are [U] planning allowances. No bench test or procurement performed.
